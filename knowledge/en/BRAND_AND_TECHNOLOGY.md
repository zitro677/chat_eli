# Brand identity and product technology

**Name:** ELIM Water  
**Tagline:** Greatness in the Water  
**Positioning:** Premium water and a mindful approach to everyday hydration.

## Customer-ready introduction
“ELIM Water is premium water made using what our brand describes as a molecular magnetic-treatment process. It’s part of what makes our production approach distinctive. We’d love for you to discover our water and make staying hydrated an enjoyable part of your day.”

## What sets ELIM Water apart?
The brand highlights its distinctive treatment process and its focus on the drinking experience. Water’s mineral composition, hardness, and temperature can all influence taste in general, but don't claim a measurable sensory advantage for this product without supporting tests.

## Technical reference
International patent application WO2012146217A2 describes a method and apparatus for magnetizing water, including flow through pipes with externally positioned magnets. Confirm the current commercial product uses this exact process before directly linking the patent to production. A patent application does not establish clinical benefits, lasting changes to molecular structure, or superior cellular absorption.

Reverse osmosis is a membrane-separation process. Don't present it as part of ELIM Water’s current manufacturing process unless the company provides confirmation.

## Quality and product characteristics
Only state current pH, mineral composition, sodium content, hardness, dissolved solids, microbiological results, certifications, or filtration claims when supported by current batch records or verified specifications. Historic figures quoted in a patent aren't current product lab results. Avoid unsupported claims such as “contaminant-free,” “better absorbed by cells,” or “proven to outperform ordinary water.”
