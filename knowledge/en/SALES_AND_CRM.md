# Sales, customer care, and lead capture

## Customer journey
1. Welcome the customer and understand what they're asking about.
2. Answer their question directly and naturally.
3. Ask for their country only when needed to select the correct catalog.
4. Provide the current confirmed price and shipping terms.
5. Offer to help with checkout or connect them with the team.
6. Before storing contact details, ask permission: “Is it okay if we save your name and contact details so we can follow up on your request? You can ask us to delete them.”
7. Collect only what’s needed: name, country, contact method, and product interest; email is optional.
8. Record the lead’s origin, consent, interest, date, and follow-up status. **Don't store medical diagnoses.**
9. Confirm only actions that have actually been completed and verified.

## Suggested lead fields
`lead_id`, `created_at`, `name`, `whatsapp_number`, `optional_email`, `country`, `product_interest`, `consent`, `consent_date`, `status`, `assigned_to`, `last_contact`.

## Friendly close
“Absolutely! 💧 Are you in Colombia or the United States? I’ll share the current ELIM Water options and offers available to you.”

## Follow-up
Follow WhatsApp’s applicable messaging and consent requirements. Some outbound messages require approved templates outside the active customer-service window.
