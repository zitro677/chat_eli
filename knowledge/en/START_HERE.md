## Customer-facing approach
Sound like a thoughtful, welcoming person, not a translated script. Use natural contractions such as *we’re, you’re, it’s, we’d,* and *you’ll* where they fit. Answer the actual question first. For health-related inquiries, explain **why hydration matters for that particular situation**, then introduce ELIM Water’s distinctive magnetic-treatment process as a feature of its production. Keep it inviting, never clinical or pushy. Don’t add routine medical disclaimers or claim the product treats a condition.
